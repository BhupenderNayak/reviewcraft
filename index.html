Frequently Asked Questions

Are these reviews 100% safe for my Google Business Profile?

Every review is posted manually by real people in our network using unique devices and IPs, never automated bots.

Can I provide the exact review text/statements I want posted?

Yes! In Step 2 of our process, you can send us the exact custom statements and comments you want shown, or give us keywords and let our writers draft natural reviews.

How long does delivery take, and can I choose my own deadline?

By default, we drip reviews gradually for maximum safety. However, with our Growth (₹27) and Premium (₹30) packages, you can select your own custom timeframe and target completion date.

What is the difference between the ₹25, ₹27, and ₹30 packages?

Starter (₹25) covers safe manual keyword-based reviews. Growth (₹27) adds location-based targeting, High Authority/Local Guide profiles, and custom delivery timelines. Premium (₹30) includes everything in Growth plus 5–10 extra free bonus reviews and a 10%–20% discount on your next order.

What happens if a review ever drops or doesn't show up?

Because we use verified high-authority human profiles and natural posting schedules, our retention rate is over 98%. If any review drops within the warranty window, we replace it free of charge.

Do I need to share my Google Business Profile password?

Never. We only need your public Google Maps business link (URL) and your preferred keywords or custom review statements.

Can you target reviewers from a specific city or region?

Yes, our Growth (₹27) and Premium (₹30) plans specifically match profiles and location references to your target city or service area.

How do I place an order and make payment?

Use the interactive review slider on our Pricing section to calculate your package cost, click the WhatsApp button to send your pre-filled order details, and our team will share the onboarding steps and UPI/bank payment options immediately.
